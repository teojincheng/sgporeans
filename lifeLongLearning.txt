Educational institutions and working professionals are in a good position to contribute to each other’s wellbeing. 
This is because polytechnics and universities can gain valuable information about the real life work environment from working professionals. 
Technology and artificial intelligence is always progressing and transforming existing jobs. 
Educational institutes can help working professionals address issues related to the trend on technology and AI. 

An adult educator who taught skillfuture courses shared that people attending such courses appreciate the fact that part or all of the financial costs related to enrolling in these courses are shouldered by the government.
However, ordinary Singaporeans wondered about the purpose of attending such classes. 
These people are putting in a lot of effort to juggle work,social and family commitments while attending classes. 
It is also undeniable that attending all these training does not guarantee an individual will find meaningful long term employment. 
It is suggested that promoting entrepreneurship and innovation may also help Singaporeans. 

A lady that wrote into the straits times forum praised the government for  introducing SkillsFuture and National Silver Academy.
She stated that there could be more options for retired and semi retired professionals.  She highlighted that there is a wider variety of fields of study today compared to the past. The population that was aforementioned have more free time in the present and like the idea of going back to school. She felt that the advertisements and brochures for institutes for higher learning mostly feature young people. 

In some other countries, senior citizens only pay half of the original tuition fees for attending university. Because Singapore has one of the fastest ageing populations in Asia, she opined that older people will likely re enter or remain in the workforce. She encourages universities to let go of ageism in their marketing.
